This policy explains how the [[APP NAME]] browser app ("the app") and its configuration server handle personal data.
You can browse without an account. We designed the app so that your browsing stays on your device.

## 1. Data that stays on your device
Browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs, shortcuts, downloads, site permissions, cookies and website data, and your settings
are stored only on your device. We never receive them. You can delete them at any time in *Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing
data*, per site in the site information panel, or by uninstalling the app.

**Private tabs** keep cookies and website data separate from normal tabs and do not save history; this data is deleted when the
last private tab is closed. Private tabs do not make you anonymous to websites, your network operator or your internet provider.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads inside the app's own screens (never inside websites). Ads are provided by Google Ireland Ltd /
Google LLC through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may process: approximate location derived from IP address, device
identifiers (Android Advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking), ad interactions, and diagnostics, for ad
delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. In the EEA/UK/Switzerland and other regions where required, we ask for your
consent through Google's consent message (UMP) before personalised ads; you can change your choice in *Settings → Ads &
subscription → Ad privacy choices*. On iOS, the tracking permission is asked separately and declining does not affect browsing.
No ad requests are made in private tabs or when you have an active ad-free subscription.
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — How Google uses data from partner apps:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) consent where required; otherwise Art. 6(1)(f).
